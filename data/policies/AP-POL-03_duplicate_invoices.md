# AP-POL-03 Duplicate Invoices

## §3.1 Duplicate detection
Before matching, every invoice is checked against posted invoices. An invoice with the same vendor and the same invoice number as a posted invoice is a duplicate, whatever the email subject or cover note says.

## §3.2 Handling duplicates
A duplicate invoice is rejected and returned to the vendor with the posting date of the original. It is never paid and never routed for approval. Three or more duplicates from one vendor in a quarter are reported to Internal Audit.
