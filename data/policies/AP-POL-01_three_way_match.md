# AP-POL-01 Three-Way Match and Tolerances

## §1.1 Price tolerance
The invoiced unit price is compared with the purchase order unit price for each line. A unit price variance of up to 2% of the PO price is within tolerance and needs no action. A variance above 2% is a price variance exception. The invoice is routed to the buyer who raised the purchase order, who must either amend the PO or reject the overcharge before the invoice can be paid.

## §1.2 Quantity tolerance
The invoiced quantity must not exceed the quantity recorded on goods receipt notes for the same PO line. There is no quantity tolerance. If the invoiced quantity is greater than the received quantity, the invoice is placed on hold and referred to Receiving until the remaining goods are receipted or the vendor issues a credit note.

## §1.3 Invoices without a purchase order
An invoice that does not quote a valid purchase order cannot be matched. It is routed to the owner of the cost centre that received the service for a non-PO approval. Repeated non-PO invoices from the same vendor are reported to Procurement monthly.
