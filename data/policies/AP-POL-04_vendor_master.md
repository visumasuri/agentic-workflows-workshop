# AP-POL-04 Vendor Master Controls

## §4.1 Bank detail changes
The bank account on an invoice must equal the bank account on the vendor master record. If it differs, the invoice is placed on hold and referred to the Vendor Master team, who verify the change by calling the vendor on the telephone number already on file. Bank details are never updated from an invoice or an email.

## §4.2 Inactive or blocked vendors
An invoice from a vendor whose status is not ACTIVE must not be paid. It is placed on hold and referred to the Vendor Master team to confirm whether the vendor should be reactivated.
