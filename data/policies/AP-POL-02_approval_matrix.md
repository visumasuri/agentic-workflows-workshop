# AP-POL-02 Approval Matrix

## §2.1 Automatic approval
A fully matched invoice with a total below 10,000 in the invoice currency may be approved for payment automatically, without human review.

## §2.2 AP Manager approval
A fully matched invoice with a total from 10,000 up to and including 50,000 requires approval by the AP Manager before posting.

## §2.3 Controller approval
A fully matched invoice with a total above 50,000 requires approval by the Financial Controller before posting. The approver must not be the person who entered or processed the invoice.
